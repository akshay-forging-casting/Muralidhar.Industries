What we do

Core manufacturing capabilities

Metalworking services for custom components and production requirements. Share your specifications and we can discuss the right process for the part.

01 / FORGING

Forged components

Forged parts designed for demanding industrial applications and manufactured to your requirements.

  • Closed-die and open-die forging
  • Upset and custom-profile forging
  • Flanges, shafts, rings and gear blanks
  • Carbon, alloy and stainless steel
  • As-forged, heat-treated or machined
02 / CASTING

Ferrous & non-ferrous castings

Cast components for complex forms, housings and other custom industrial applications.

  • Sand, shell, investment and die casting
  • Grey iron, SG iron and cast steel
  • Aluminium, brass and bronze / gunmetal
  • Pump casings, housings and valve bodies
  • Rough-cast, fettled or machined
03 / MACHINING & FINISH

Precision machining

Secondary and finish operations to bring forged, cast and stock material to final specification.

  • CNC turning and VMC milling
  • Boring, drilling, tapping and threading
  • Facing, slotting and keyways
  • Cylindrical and surface grinding
  • Heat treatment and protective coatings
